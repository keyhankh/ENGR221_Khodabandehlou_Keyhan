2.3 Adding Food Pseudocode

add_food():
    Pick a random row
    Pick a random column
    Get a cell at that row and column
    If cell is empty: 
        Change cell to Food
        Add cell to the food list
        Decrease the number of empty cells by 1

eat_food(cell):
    Remove the cell from the food list
    Increase score by 1
    Increase number of empty cells by 1
