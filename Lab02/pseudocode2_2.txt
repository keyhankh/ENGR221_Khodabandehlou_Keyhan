2.2 Player Movement Pseudocode

move_player_right():
    Get the cell to the right of the player
    If that cell exists:
        Move the player to that cell

move_player_left():
    Get the cell to the left of the player
    If that cell exists:
        Move the player to that cell

move_player_up():
    Get the cell above the player
    If that cell exists:
        Move the player to that cell

move_player_down():
    Get the cell below the player
    If that cell exists:
        Move the player to that cell
