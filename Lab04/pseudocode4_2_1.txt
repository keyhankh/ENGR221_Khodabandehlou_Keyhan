move_player_to_cell(cell):

    if cell is outside the board or cell is part of the body:
        set game to over

    else if cell has food: 
        change current player cell into body
        change food cell into the new player
        add the new player cell to the front of the body list
        remove the food from the food list
        add 1 to the overall score

    else if cell is empty: 
        change the current player cell into body
        change the empty cell into the new player
        add the new player cell to the front of the body list
        remove the last cell from the body list
        change that old tial cell into empty
